# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to get a game to run.

## What is expected

- Disagree with the idea, never with the person.
- Say what you saw, what you expected and what you ran.
- Keep requests for games, keys or firmware out of this project; they are refused and the thread is closed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove anything that breaks this, and may block anyone who keeps breaking it. Report a problem privately through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
